# Code of Conduct

This project expects respectful, technical, and direct collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in discussions, reviews, and issues.
- Focus criticism on the code, design, or evidence, not on the person.
- Assume good intent, but be precise when pointing out problems.
- Keep feedback actionable.
- Accept that maintainers may reject ideas that do not fit the product direction.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior
- Repeated bad-faith arguing after a decision is made
- Publishing private information without permission
- Spamming, trolling, or disruptive behavior

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban from the project spaces.

## Reporting

If something crosses the line, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub.
